# foia-request-tracker-skill

## Description
Automates the drafting, statutory deadline tracking, exemption challenge preparation, and agency log maintenance for FOIA (Federal) and state public records requests.

## Instructions
1. **Request Drafting & Formatting:**
   - Draft formal public records requests referencing applicable statutes (e.g., 5 U.S.C. § 552 for Federal FOIA or state-specific sunshine laws).
   - Clearly delineate requested records using narrow date ranges, specific custodian titles, and precise search terms to avoid "unduly burdensome" rejections.
   - Include statutory fee waiver justifications under 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) demonstrating news media status and public interest value.

2. **Deadline & Calendar Tracking:**
   - Automatically calculate statutory response windows (e.g., 20 business days for federal FOIA).
   - Log request submission date, tracking number, agency contact, and target determination date into a central Google Sheet.
   - Generate calendar reminders for follow-ups at Day 10 (status check), Day 20 (determination due), and Day 30 (constructive denial threshold).

3. **Exemption Audit & Appeal Preparation:**
   - When an agency responds with redactions or withholdings, analyze cited exemptions (e.g., Exemption 5 b(5) deliberative process, Exemption 7(C) personal privacy).
   - Draft administrative appeal letters challenging improper withholdings, citing relevant circuit court precedents and arguing for segregability under the FOIA Improvement Act.
   - Maintain a standardized log of agency response patterns to track chronic disclosure delays or systemic non-compliance.
