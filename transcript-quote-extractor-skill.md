# transcript-quote-extractor-skill

## Description
Processes long-form audio/video transcripts from investigative interviews, press conferences, and court hearings into organized, timestamped quote banks and thematic debriefs.

## Instructions
1. **Verbatim Quote Extraction:**
   - Extract high-value, direct verbatim quotes from raw transcripts, preserving exact phrasing without altering grammar or meaning.
   - Categorize quotes by key themes (e.g., Admissions, Policy Changes, Denials, Financial Figures, Timeline Conflicts).

2. **Context & Verification Anchor:**
   - Attach precise timestamp links (e.g., `[00:14:32]`) and speaker attribution to every extracted quote.
   - Note audio clarity or speaker hesitation markers (e.g., `[unclear]`, `[pause]`) where verbatim precision is critical for legal clearance.

3. **Executive Interview Debrief:**
   - Generate a 1-page summary highlighting new admissions, contradictions against prior statements, and key narrative arcs.
   - Format a clean "Quote Bank" section ready for reporters to copy directly into story drafts.
