# osint-investigator-skill

## Description
Executes structured Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) workflows to map corporate registries, domain ownership histories, public filings, and digital footprints for investigative reporting.

## Instructions
1. **Corporate & Asset Mapping:**
   - Cross-reference subjects against SEC EDGAR filings, state business registries, international corporate registries (e.g., UK Companies House, offshore leaks databases).
   - Extract registered agent names, incorporation dates, parent company structures, and officer histories to identify shell company networks.

2. **Digital Footprint Analysis:**
   - Query domain WHOIS history, DNS records, subdomains, and historical IP allocations for target websites.
   - Retrieve archived snapshots from the Wayback Machine and digital archives to document deleted web pages, altered press releases, or removed staff bios.

3. **Dossier Compilation & Chain of Custody:**
   - Compile all findings into a structured Google Doc "OSINT Verification Dossier".
   - Include direct URL links, archive links (Wayback/archive.today), timestamped screenshots, and document IDs for every factual entry.
   - Flag gaps in public record trails where formal FOIA requests or court record searches are required.
